What is First Principles Thinking?
(第一性原理思維)

Elon Musk's core framework: Never accept things "just because people have always done it that way." Strip any problem down to its fundamental, unalterable truths (physics & raw facts), then rebuild the solution from scratch.

1. Analogy vs. First Principles
❌ 類比思維 (Analogy) "Looking at what competitors do and copying or making 5% tweaks."
🎯 第一性原理 (First Principles) "Asking: What are the base physical components & raw facts?"
2. Real-World Examples
🚀 Example A: SpaceX Rocket Cost
Analogy: Contractors charge $60M.
First Principle: Raw materials (aluminum, titanium, carbon fiber) cost only ~$1.2M (2%)! The other 98% was process waste.
⌚ Example B: Watch Manufacturing (Oro Time)
Analogy: Vendor quotes $45 for steel case.
First Principle: Raw 316L steel (~85g) = $0.35 + CNC time (4.2 min) = $1.80 + hand polish = $0.90. Base cost is under $6.
🏢 Example C: Bureaucratic Approvals
Analogy: 7 managers sign every PO.
First Principle: Having 7 busy people rubber-stamp dilutes accountability. Automate ERP limits: <$5,000 auto-approved; >$5,000 one owner signs.
