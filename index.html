What is First Principles Thinking?
(第一性原理思維)

Elon Musk's core thinking framework: Never accept things "just because people have always done it that way." Strip any problem down to its fundamental, unalterable truths (physics & raw facts), then rebuild the solution from scratch.

1. Analogy vs. First Principles
❌ 類比思維 (Analogy) "Looking at what competitors do and copying or making 5% tweaks."
Result: Slow, incremental, stuck in industry customs.
🎯 第一性原理 (First Principles) "Ignoring all existing practices. Asking: What are the base physical components & costs?"
Result: 10x cost reduction & game-changing innovation.
2. Real-World Examples
🚀 Example A: SpaceX Rocket Cost
Analogy Thinking: Aerospace contractors sell a rocket for $60M because "rockets have always been expensive."
First Principle Thinking: What is a rocket made of? Aerospace-grade aluminum, titanium, copper, carbon fiber.
👉 Raw material cost is only ~$1.2M (2% of the price!)
Musk realized the other 98% is process waste. SpaceX built their own factory and created reusable rockets.
⌚ Example B: Watch & Jewellery Manufacturing (Oro Time)
Analogy Thinking: "Suppliers quote $45 for this stainless steel case because that's the market quote."
First Principle Thinking: What is the raw 316L steel weight? ~85g = $0.35 steel. CNC time = 4.2 min = $1.80 power/depreciation. Polish = 3 min = $0.90 labor.
👉 The physical cost floor is under $6. Everything above that is supplier markup or inefficient tooling!
🏢 Example C: Management Approval Chains
Analogy Thinking: "A purchase order needs 7 managers' signatures because that's our company policy."
First Principle Thinking: What is the true goal? To prevent financial theft/error. Having 7 busy people rubber-stamp dilutes accountability to zero.
👉 Solution: Automate limits in ERP. Under $5,000 = 1 click auto-approved; Over $5,000 = 1 single responsible owner signs.
